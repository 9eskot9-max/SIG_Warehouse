# People configuration reconciliation and staged safety fix

Status: **STAGED, NOT DEPLOYED**. On 4 November 2026 the live People project-assignment list was observed empty and People routing revision 1 had been published. The owner-provided review reports further live counts and data conflicts; these are leads for a fresh read-only inventory, not verified cleanup instructions. No employee, account, phone, Project, cycle or routing data is changed by this proposal.

## Immediate safety issue

`pc_task_queue` and the status projection in `pc_journal` use `pc_review_routing.load_matrix()` and the published People matrix. `pc_journal._context()` reads the static `pc_review_scopes.json` for reviewer authorization and native transaction permission hooks. `people_routing.publish` validates a compiled scope but does not persist or use that scope for authorization. Editing the matrix can therefore change task recipients without changing who can read or confirm the receipt. The staged fix blocks the publish endpoint even for System Manager and renders the People routing editor read-only. It does not change current access or align the readers. **The production site remains editable and exposed until this fix is deployed.** A later release should use a single coherent, validated revision for routing and permission decisions and check both additions and removals before unfreezing. It must address request consistency, cache behavior, rollback and invalid/missing configuration without widening Finance or reviewer access.

## Family, teams and people

The target model is **family → team → members**, with a leader and effective dates. A team may work on multiple Projects in one family; a Project may have several teams. A person's PM, coordinator, team leader or member role is explicit and may vary by family/team/time. Exact Project/PO responsibility (including a PM) stays a separately evidenced relationship. Do not populate all projects in a family as individual assignments for every employee. Retire or revise the existing single-active-Team-Leader-per-Project validation only after an approved migration and comparison of its consumers and permissions.

Use **5G / Colocation** as the unified human-readable family label, retaining compatibility with existing `5G`, `Colocation`, `colo` and `colocation` values and stable identifiers until migration is verified. Existing Operations `_family()` already maps 5G and Colocation to `Colocation`; that alone does not normalize Project records, workbook exports, planner or PC consumers. Inventory metadata options, Project family fields (`custom_family` and `custom_project_family` where present), Excel Setup PO projections and RV80 contracts, SIG Site Cycle links and task routing, Operations roster/aliases, PC groups and additional exceptions, planner teams and weekly PM scope before changing any label or key. Preserve historic values and read aliases throughout a versioned migration; no inferred family for blank or conflicting Projects. Do not rename a key or rewrite historical cycle data as a display-only change.

## Final design decisions and assessment of review

- **Freeze first, then align.** The reviewer is correct that independent task and authorization sources can diverge. PR #81 stages the freeze only. The durable fix is one immutable, validated, revisioned routing policy from which task targeting and clearance authorization derive the same employee-ID scope. Publish must calculate and present effective reviewer/access additions and removals, honor self-review exclusion and Finance exceptions, then atomically publish a coherent revision. Cache/version invalidation, concurrency and rollback must be designed before activation. The scope remains bounded by Frappe roles and existing DocType/record permissions; a team relationship itself never grants broad ERP access.
- **Family → team → dated members is the primary operational model.** A team has a stable ID, one effective leader at a time, a family and an effective-dated membership history. A person may belong to more than one team where the periods and roles permit. Teams may serve multiple projects in a family, and multiple teams may serve one project. Do not create employee/Project rows by expanding a family. A team/project exception or assignment can be recorded when an exact link is genuinely needed. PM and coordinator duties remain explicit family or Project responsibilities rather than being conflated with team membership. Remove the one-leader-per-Project restriction as part of the governed change after validating historical data, downstream consumers and access.
- **One family label, compatible identifiers.** Present `5G / Colocation` as one family. Normalize old labels at boundaries through a stable canonical family ID and an explicit alias table. Keep existing PC group keys and Project data compatible until a reviewed migration and all consumer checks pass; never fill blank families or rewrite cycle history by assumption. A project-family decision changes what Operations queues include, so compare affected cycles and recipients before publication.
- **Default petty-cash routing, explicit policy exceptions.** The employee's team/family proposes a PC review group where an approved family mapping exists. Keep an explicit override for exceptions and for non-team employees such as Finance and Warehouse. Distinguish inherited values from overrides in the UI and show who will review the person's receipts. The reviewer is right that the two existing group columns are redundant for the current 58 rows; use one operational family plus a derived PC group with a visible override instead of asking users to maintain two identical values. Preserve the two additional Incident scopes and any other approved exceptions. The leader is not automatically the reviewer, and team membership is not independent authorization to confirm receipts.
- **Keep People in Excel Setup, clarify what saves.** The criticism that one generic save button obscures three save mechanisms is valid. Keep the shared interface the owner requested, but give workbook configuration, People routing and directly edited ERP records clear names, save/publish actions, revision/status labels and unsaved-change handling. Allow HR, Operations and Finance to manage their own approved areas through narrowly defined permissions; snapshot and test effective roles and linked records before any change. JSON import can remain a migration tool, not the normal management workflow.
- **Do not treat inferred cleanup as fact.** An unassigned PC group, Site Operations department or missing phone does not identify a person's leader. The owner review's counts and alleged duplicate/numbering anomalies are useful investigation leads; reconcile live IDs and source evidence before merging employees, changing phones, assigning teams, or altering accounts. Team membership may provide a proposed family but does not verify a phone. Reporter maps resolve phone-to-person; existing field feeds may also use Employee phone and other sources, so seven explicit map rows do not prove that only seven people can report. Finance verifies custody accounts and AM beneficiary links; missing or differently numbered accounts are exceptions for review, not a reason to create or renumber accounts automatically.
- **Stage the rollout in dependency order.** (1) Deploy the narrowly scoped freeze with owner authorization and permission gate. (2) Inventory live data and prepare a per-person and per-project reconciliation report with provenance, conflicts and current task/clearance scopes. (3) Build the shared routing/authorization policy and demonstrate before/after parity and approved exceptions. (4) Build family/team/member records and the People setup UX, migrate verified memberships and connect Operations, PC and planner/reporting consumers deliberately. (5) Rehearse migration and rollback, compare visibility and permissions, exercise actual-user workflows, then unfreeze and release only after required acceptance. Weekly PM check recipients and family/Project coverage remain a separate explicitly configured decision; existing code consumes an employee-to-Project scope, so they cannot be assumed from team membership.

# People configuration reconciliation and staged safety fix

Status: **STAGED, NOT DEPLOYED**. On 4 November 2026 the live People project-assignment list was observed empty and People routing revision 1 had been published. The owner-provided review reports further live counts and data conflicts; these are leads for a fresh read-only inventory, not verified cleanup instructions. No employee, account, phone, Project, cycle or routing data is changed by this proposal.

## Immediate safety issue

`pc_task_queue` and the status projection in `pc_journal` use `pc_review_routing.load_matrix()` and the published People matrix. `pc_journal._context()` reads the static `pc_review_scopes.json` for reviewer authorization and native transaction permission hooks. `people_routing.publish` validates a compiled scope but does not persist or use that scope for authorization. Editing the matrix can therefore change task recipients without changing who can read or confirm the receipt. The staged fix blocks the publish endpoint even for System Manager and renders the People routing editor read-only. It does not change current access or align the readers. **The production site remains editable and exposed until this fix is deployed.** A later release should use a single coherent, validated revision for routing and permission decisions and check both additions and removals before unfreezing. It must address request consistency, cache behavior, rollback and invalid/missing configuration without widening Finance or reviewer access.

## Family, teams and people

The target model is **family → team → members**, with a leader and effective dates. A team may work on multiple Projects in one family; a Project may have several teams. A person's PM, coordinator, team leader or member role is explicit and may vary by family/team/time. Exact Project/PO responsibility (including a PM) stays a separately evidenced relationship. Do not populate all projects in a family as individual assignments for every employee. Retire or revise the existing single-active-Team-Leader-per-Project validation only after an approved migration and comparison of its consumers and permissions.

Use **5G / Colocation** as the unified human-readable family label, retaining compatibility with existing `5G`, `Colocation`, `colo` and `colocation` values and stable identifiers until migration is verified. Existing Operations `_family()` already maps 5G and Colocation to `Colocation`; that alone does not normalize Project records, workbook exports, planner or PC consumers. Inventory metadata options, Project family fields (`custom_family` and `custom_project_family` where present), Excel Setup PO projections and RV80 contracts, SIG Site Cycle links and task routing, Operations roster/aliases, PC groups and additional exceptions, planner teams and weekly PM scope before changing any label or key. Preserve historic values and read aliases throughout a versioned migration; no inferred family for blank or conflicting Projects. Do not rename a key or rewrite historical cycle data as a display-only change.

## Final design decisions and assessment of review

- **Freeze first, then align.** The reviewer is correct that independent task and authorization sources can diverge. PR #81 stages the freeze only. The durable fix is one immutable, validated, revisioned routing policy from which task targeting and clearance authorization derive the same employee-ID scope. Publish must calculate and present effective reviewer/access additions and removals, honor self-review exclusion and Finance exceptions, then atomically publish a coherent revision. Cache/version invalidation, concurrency and rollback must be designed before activation. The scope remains bounded by Frappe roles and existing DocType/record permissions; a team relationship itself never grants broad ERP access.
- **Family → team → dated members is the primary operational model.** A team has a stable ID, one effective leader at a time, a family and an effective-dated membership history. A person may belong to more than one team where the periods and roles permit. Teams may serve multiple projects in a family, and multiple teams may serve one project. Do not create employee/Project rows by expanding a family. A team/project exception or assignment can be recorded when an exact link is genuinely needed. PM and coordinator duties remain explicit family or Project responsibilities rather than being conflated with team membership. Remove the one-leader-per-Project restriction as part of the governed change after validating historical data, downstream consumers and access.
- **One family label, compatible identifiers.** Present `5G / Colocation` as one family. Normalize old labels at boundaries through a stable canonical family ID and an explicit alias table. Keep existing PC group keys and Project data compatible until a reviewed migration and all consumer checks pass; never fill blank families or rewrite cycle history by assumption. A project-family decision changes what Operations queues include, so compare affected cycles and recipients before publication.
- **Default petty-cash routing, explicit policy exceptions.** The employee's team/family proposes a PC review group where an approved family mapping exists. Keep an explicit override for exceptions and for non-team employees such as Finance and Warehouse. Distinguish inherited values from overrides in the UI and show who will review the person's receipts. The reviewer is right that the two existing group columns are redundant for the current 58 rows; use one operational family plus a derived PC group with a visible override instead of asking users to maintain two identical values. Preserve the two additional Incident scopes and any other approved exceptions. The leader is not automatically the reviewer, and team membership is not independent authorization to confirm receipts.
- **Keep People in Excel Setup, clarify what saves.** The criticism that one generic save button obscures three save mechanisms is valid. Keep the shared interface the owner requested, but give workbook configuration, People routing and directly edited ERP records clear names, save/publish actions, revision/status labels and unsaved-change handling. Allow HR, Operations and Finance to manage their own approved areas through narrowly defined permissions; snapshot and test effective roles and linked records before any change. JSON import can remain a migration tool, not the normal management workflow.
- **Do not treat inferred cleanup as fact.** An unassigned PC group, Site Operations department or missing phone does not identify a person's leader. The owner review's counts and alleged duplicate/numbering anomalies are useful investigation leads; reconcile live IDs and source evidence before merging employees, changing phones, assigning teams, or altering accounts. Team membership may provide a proposed family but does not verify a phone. Reporter maps resolve phone-to-person; existing field feeds may also use Employee phone and other sources, so seven explicit map rows do not prove that only seven people can report. Finance verifies custody accounts and AM beneficiary links; missing or differently numbered accounts are exceptions for review, not a reason to create or renumber accounts automatically.
- **Stage the rollout in dependency order.** (1) Deploy the narrowly scoped freeze with owner authorization and permission gate. (2) Inventory live data and prepare a per-person and per-project reconciliation report with provenance, conflicts and current task/clearance scopes. (3) Build the shared routing/authorization policy and demonstrate before/after parity and approved exceptions. (4) Build family/team/member records and the People setup UX, migrate verified memberships and connect Operations, PC and planner/reporting consumers deliberately. (5) Rehearse migration and rollback, compare visibility and permissions, exercise actual-user workflows, then unfreeze and release only after required acceptance. Weekly PM check recipients and family/Project coverage remain a separate explicitly configured decision; existing code consumes an employee-to-Project scope, so they cannot be assumed from team membership.

## Cross-system decisions after code and master review — 5 November 2026

### Ownership and minimum data

Keep Employee as the identity anchor and Project as the canonical PO/project record. Add only the missing operational relations: stable team ID, effective-dated leader and member links, family, explicit internal PM/coordinator scope, and controlled PC overrides/reviewer policy. Expose them through the existing Excel Setup People area using owning records and permission-checked APIs. Reuse existing `SIG Field Reporter Map`, `Employee.cell_number`, `Employee.custom_custody_account`, and native Project/PO setup rather than copying their contents into another all-purpose People JSON. Keep history and business ownership for each. The current Excel setup `teams` dataset is an ERP planner roster, not a complete team membership model; migrate or adapt it deliberately, not by creating a competing second live roster.

### Confirmed code interactions and contracts

- **Project family:** Excel Setup `_linked_masters` and `excel_setup_v3` prefer `custom_project_family`, then `custom_family`; Operations `_cycle_view` and field-feed `_check_capex2_scope` currently use `custom_family` or legacy cycle text. Consequently two populated conflicting fields can route the same Project differently. Inventory both fields and sources first, define one reviewed canonical mapping plus conflict diagnostics, and preserve each existing consumer's output until a staged before/after comparison is accepted. The alias `5G / Colocation` must also be recognized by field-feed scope guards; otherwise a label-only update can change a Capex2 safety decision. Keep workbook V3 values/PO identifiers compatible with RV80; do not equate 34 configured workbook Project rows with the full ERP Project universe.
- **Client contact versus internal PM:** the existing `SIG Project PM Assignment` accessed by `excel_setup_v3` uses a `tawal_contact` and reconciles it with `Project.custom_default_tawal_contact`. It is client-contact configuration, not evidence of an internal Employee PM. Retain that contract. Internal PM and coordinator family/Project responsibility must refer to Employee IDs and be reconciled separately. The weekly PM job currently reads active employee-to-Project `SIG PM Weekly Scope`; construct or adapt that scope only from explicitly approved recipients and Project coverage, with no automatic WhatsApp sends or change of mode.
- **Planner compatibility:** `field_planner_api._team_setup` reads the published `SIG Excel Setup.configuration_json` teams and positions/visits/sessions are currently matched by leader Employee ID. `SIG Planner Position.validate` also checks that same configuration. Keep an adapter or a controlled migration that gives both the planner and its validator one consistent enabled-team view; preserve the current `Project` / `Incident` archetypes, enabled state, capacity calculation and API shape. A stable team ID enables leader changes, but historical visit/session and position records must retain their original leader. Do not automatically transfer an old leader's last location to a new leader; define and validate a handover rule before using it as a team's starting position. Do not infer live team assignments from old plans or use team membership as proof of a visit.
- **Field identity:** the field feed combines Employee phones with explicit Field Reporter Maps and applies a scope guard to Capex2; the no-visit job draws on observed visits/messages and maps. Preserve these mechanisms and collision handling. A team roster should enrich an identified person's context, never silently reattribute old visits/messages or enable notifications. Missing and duplicate phones remain review cases.
- **PC and permissions:** the task scheduler makes ToDos by reviewer group and user, excludes the sender's own receipts, and closes obsolete ToDos when it syncs. The clearance hooks independently resolve sender and reviewer and rely on normal Frappe permissions; Finance and audit paths have deliberately broader handling. A new compiled policy must be generated from stable Employee IDs rather than matching reviewer names. Resolve Employee/User ambiguity, missing reviewers, unassigned senders and exceptions explicitly; preserve Finance fallback and independent-review behavior. Take one revision per request/job, publish the full validated policy and effective scopes atomically, and give workers a defined revision transition so one scheduler run cannot close/create ToDos from a partial policy. Record the applied revision in audit/evidence and compare old/new task recipients, transaction/JE query and document permissions, confirm actions and existing open ToDos before unfreezing. Roles and native DocPerm remain a separate required boundary.
- **Multiple memberships:** where an employee has several active teams/families, a single default PC group is ambiguous. Resolve it through an explicit Finance-approved effective-dated PC policy/override (and any approved extra reviewer scopes), not by arbitrary first-team order. The currently observed equal group values do not prove that future exceptions are unnecessary.

### Final family and PM design

- Continue using **`5G` as the canonical machine/export value** and show **5G / Colocation** as the user-facing family label. - ERP owns an effective-dated **TAWAL client PM default per family**, referencing an active existing SIG TAWAL Contact. Permit a deliberate PO exception and preserve existing Project field/TAWAL assignment semantics through a compatible effective `default_contact` projection in the V3 bundle. Do not copy a family contact onto every new PO. Classify existing PO defaults as family inheritance or genuine exceptions with provenance before migration; do not silently erase them. Support an explicit inherit/exception/cleared state and enforce one applicable family default per effective date. Invalid/inactive/missing defaults or conflicting sources surface a clear review error rather than choosing another person. Internal Employee PM and weekly-review recipients remain separately configured.
- RV80 resolves **per-batch run override → PO exception → family default**. For mixed PO batches, show effective contact and source for every PO; if different, allow the operator to choose one active contact for that batch or split it. Validate every output before creating files. Apply the selected TAWAL contact consistently to CSV PM name/mobile/email and the relevant MOP PM placeholder, without changing ERP defaults or other batches. The TCN worksheet TMS PM and its implementer mapping are separate inputs; do not replace them as a side effect. Reset override state after completion/cancel/error, and pin the contact ID, name, mobile, email, effective source/date, PO/batch and setup revision/hash in an auditable export snapshot so later contact edits, family changes or reprints do not silently rewrite history. Define repeat/export behavior before coding; effective dates alone do not preserve generated output history.
- V3 bundles today return a resolved `default_contact` per enabled canonical PO, active contact directory and a hash of the projected data; ExcelSetupSync reads that field. Prefer maintaining that contract for older workbook adapters while optionally adding provenance in a compatible versioned extension. Verify that a changed family default/contact causes the bundle hash and RV80 Sync From ERP to deliver the updated effective value even when the Excel Setup revision is unchanged. Confirm active-workbook import, cache refresh, both outputs, multi-batch overrides and cancel/error cleanup before calling the full path complete.

### PC scope simplification

Ensure the error handling does not inadvertently remove broad Finance access or cause an unintended permission grant. Name-based reviewer resolution currently underlies compilation: detect duplicate/missing names and prepare a deliberate move to stable Employee IDs. The source-owned matrix currently compiles exactly to the source-owned scope file for 58 employees; this is not proof that a later live published revision matches the file. ## Consolidated ERP and RV80 revamp — final recommended scope, 5 November 2026

### User experience and ownership

Keep the existing Excel Setup interface as the single entry point, with clearly named sections and save actions: Projects and families (Operations), TAWAL contacts and family defaults/PO exceptions (Operations), People and teams (HR/Operations), petty-cash reviewer rules and custody exceptions (Finance), and workbook mappings. Use permission-checked owning ERP records; do not duplicate personal or financial details in workbook exports. Show inherited versus explicit values, source and unresolved conflicts. ### Canonical identities and compatibility

- Employee and Project remain authoritative. A stable team ID has effective-dated leader/member links; multiple teams can work a Project and one team can serve multiple Projects. Family membership alone does not create exact Project rows or grant access. Internal PM/coordinator responsibilities and weekly-review coverage are explicit, distinct from the client TAWAL contact.
- Present the family as **5G / Colocation**. Use one canonical family identity; preserve `5G` as the legacy machine value on contracts that require it until their consumers are upgraded. Normalize `5G`, `Colocation`, `colo` and the new label at documented boundaries. Do not bulk rename legacy Projects, Site Cycles, Google sheet names, evidence or template files. Use a shared resolver with explicit conflicting-field diagnostics; blank families stay unresolved. Compare cycle routing, field-feed Capex2 guards, planner filters, workbook values and Tags matching before a change is activated.

The family default references the existing active contact directory; existing per-PO defaults are reconciled before classifying them as inheritance or exception.
